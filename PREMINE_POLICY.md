## Controls

- Publish all premine addresses before mainnet.
- Separate allocations into distinct wallets.
- Prefer multisignature or documented approval controls where technically practical.
- Publish material transfers and their purpose.
- Do not represent the premine as a personal unrestricted founder grant.
- Reapprove this policy after legal, tax, accounting, and securities review.
